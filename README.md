# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Title

**HR Analytics & Workforce Attrition Dashboard**

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

An interactive, data-driven HR analytics solution designed to evaluate workforce demographics, analyze employee attrition patterns, and assess job satisfaction across departments. Built with Power BI, this dashboard helps HR leaders and management pinpoint high-attrition job roles, understand demographic drivers, and make informed talent retention decisions.

## 3. Tech Stack

* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation & Modeling:** Power Query (ETL pipeline)
* **Analytical Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for workforce metrics, attrition percentages, and satisfaction scores
* **Data Storage / Input:** Flat Files / CSV

## 4. Data Source

* HR Employee Attrition & Demographic Dataset

## 5. Features & Highlights

### 📊 Executive Workforce KPIs

* **Employee Summary:** Real-time tracking of **Total Employees (1,470)**, **Active Employees (1,233)**, and **Total Attrition (237)**.


* **Workforce Metrics:** Monitoring overall **Attrition Rate (16.12%)**, **Average Age (37 years)**, and **Average Monthly Pay ($6,503)**.



### 👥 Demographic & Age Breakdown

* **Gender & Age Distribution:** Detailed workforce breakdown across age buckets (Under 25, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, Over 55) segmented by male and female employees.


* **Attrition Trend by Age:** Line chart tracking specific age-based attrition spikes, showing elevated turnover around age 28 to 31.



### 🏢 Department & Job Role Attrition

* **Department-Wise Attrition:** Donut chart breakdown showing highest turnover in **R&D (133 employees / 56.12%)**, followed by **Sales (92 / 38.82%)** and **HR (12 / 5.06%)**.


* **High-Risk Roles:** Bar chart ranking attrition rates by job title, led by **Sales Representatives (39.76%)**, **Laboratory Technicians (23.94%)**, and **Human Resources (23.08%)**.



### 🎓 Education Field & Satisfaction Metrics

* **Attrition by Education Field:** Funnel view displaying employee departure across disciplines led by Life Sciences (89), Medical (63), Marketing (35), and Technical Degrees (32).


* **Job Satisfaction Matrix:** Heatmap matrix evaluating satisfaction levels (1–4 rating scale) across job roles like Sales Executive, Research Scientist, and Laboratory Technician.



### 🎛️ Dynamic Interactive Filtering

* **Education Slicers:** Top-level filters for Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Doctoral Degree, High School, and Master's Degree.


* **Demographic & Travel Slicers:** Side-panel buttons filtering by Gender (Female/Male), Marital Status (Divorced, Married, Single), and Business Travel frequency (Non-Travel, Travel_Frequently, Travel_Rarely).
